# Venue Roster — who lives where, now that the venues are real

*Sep 25 2026. An audit of all 100 characters against the twelve racing venues as they were
actually built, because `achievements.md` (Aug 5) placed characters against venue specs
that have since changed. Companion to `achievements.md` and `roster-ranking.md`.*

**The template being filled** (settled Sep 24): five characters per venue, one per rung —
**1 First win · 2 Mechanic · 3 Explorer · 4 Target time · 5 Four stars**, plus an optional
**Wildlife** rung when a venue's animals earn one (the Cove has six). The rung-5
character must be a +3 boat or better. Species rule: real wildlife of the venue, at least
part aquatic.

## Species decisions

Six characters sit at a venue their species doesn't live at. None is fatal for a kids'
game; each is a question of whether to relabel, re-home or accept.

1. **Chomp — saltwater crocodile, at a Louisiana bayou.** Salties are Indo-Pacific; the
   bayou is alligator country, and the venue is named for Chomp's kin. Relabel Chomp as an
   **American alligator** (check the snout — gators are broad and U-shaped). Then Beau, the
   benched alligator, is a duplicate, and rung 5 wants a different new character — the
   **anhinga** (*Quill*, once planned for Flamingo Reach) is a true bayou bird.
2. **Snag — hellbender, on an Alaska salmon river.** Hellbenders are Appalachian and
   Ozark. The Pacific Northwest's own giant is the **coastal giant salamander**, and
   relabelling Snag as one keeps the "older than the river" story. *Decided Sep 25 2026 (Wes): Snag stays a hellbender — "it's unique".*
3. **Petal — roseate spoonbill, on the Wadden Sea.** Roseates are American. The Wadden
   holds over a quarter of Europe's **Eurasian spoonbills** — but they are white, and
   Petal's pink is her identity. Either accept a pink visitor, or recolour.
4. **Skitter — Atlantic mudskipper, on the Wadden Sea.** There are no mudskippers in
   Europe. He is the icon of mudflats everywhere; accept, or swap for a Wadden animal
   (lugworm, shore crab, flounder).
5. **Ember — firefish; Torch — fire salamander, at Emberfall.** Both are there for their
   names. The firefish is an Indo-Pacific reef dartfish; the fire salamander is a European
   woodland amphibian (its larvae are aquatic, so the rule is only just met). If Emberfall
   leans Galápagos, the true locals are the marine iguana (Basalt, already planned), the
   Sally Lightfoot crab, the blue-footed booby (Stomp), the flightless cormorant and the
   Galápagos penguin.
6. **Pearl — Pacific oyster.** A Pacific oyster is the food oyster; pearls come from the
   **black-lip pearl oyster** of the Polynesian atolls. A relabel, not a redraw. *Done Sep 25 2026 (Wes): roster
   creature and the manifest subject relabelled; the shipped portrait stands.*

**Accepted as designed, noted once:** Glacier Sound mixes the poles on purpose (penguins,
krill and a polar bear); Glowtide's deep-sea cast (seadevil, vampire squid) is there for
mood, not range; Drift is a temperate Pacific sea nettle — Monterey's most famous jelly —
in a tropical strait; Lunker is a southern bass in a north-woods lake (smallmouth is the
boreal one); Talon at Redrock is a winter visitor (Sockeye Run's ambient eagle is truer, but
you placed him on the canyon's apex on purpose); and the Cove's ambient **pelicans** are not
New England birds.

## Standing rules set Sep 25 2026

- **Nothing is hidden.** Every objective says exactly what to do — on the locked silhouette,
  on the venue card's objectives list and on the unlock card. (Splat and Knot were un-hidden.)
- **Four animal types per venue, except the Sailing School's pond** (js/wildlife.js), each a
  scenery system that an objective can use (Wes, Sep 25 2026; it was three until then). A
  venue may carry more than five characters when its animals earn them. As of Sep 25:
  - **Cove:** gulls, porpoises, pelicans, harbour seals.
  - **Lake:** loons, moose, beavers, jumping bass.
  - **Lagoon:** green turtles, eagle rays, blacktip sharks, tangs.
  - **Bayou:** alligators, egrets, anhingas, bullfrogs.
- **Target time** = the mean of Wes's recorded trajectories at the venue × 1.1, rounded UP
  to the next 5 s — `node regatta/eval/set_venue_targets.js [trajDir] --write`. A venue whose
  trajectories don't match today's course (leg count, or a mean outside 0.95–1.45× the
  path best) gets no target until new ones are recorded. New trajectories come after the
  achievements pass.
- **Time limit** = 10:00 on every course (`course.cutoff: 600`, written by the same script).

## How a venue gets done — lessons from the first three (Sep 25 2026)

The Cove, the School and the Lake were each **designed with Wes first, then built**. What that
taught, for the ten venues left (the full workflow is the `venue-objectives` skill):

- **Read the venue as built, not the spec.** The Lake's course is a loop round an
  archipelago, not the spec's windward/leeward. Its real question is a 2 kn glass patch
  around mark 3, and that became the mechanic rung.
- **Measure every threshold against the autopilot and the fleet before proposing it.**
  - The glass pass at 2 kn came free with any finish, so it is 4.5 kn.
  - Crossing a ship's bow within 3 lengths was never free: the autopilot comes no closer
    than 211 units.
  - A threshold belongs where it takes deliberate sailing.
- **The template bends to the venue.** The School forces auto trim, so four stars made no
  sense there: its fifth rung is a clean report card. A venue whose animals earn more than
  one objective carries a Wildlife rung.
- **Wes picks the spot for an animal.** Gull rock shape-36, the moose's lily bed shape-39, the
  turtles' log (he placed it), so offer ids rather than deciding.
- **The animals are half the venue's charm and are judged by eye.** Research them from
  overhead and drone references first. Size them by [scale.md](scale.md) before drawing.
  Their water (wakes, rings, splashes, underwater bodies, shadows) follows
  [race-view.md §10.6](race-view.md). The pond and lake animals were redone twice because
  those steps were skipped.
- **Characters ship through the pipeline:** a manifest `portrait` entry for the prompt, then
  `art/ship_portrait.py` for the delivery (the `ship-character` skill). Check a new hull
  against the venue's other hulls: Diver's gold would have twinned Bask's boat, so Diver is
  black.
- **Golden traces are not re-recorded per character** (Wes's call); each roster entry moves
  the fleet draws, so they are re-recorded once the roster settles.
