# Roster Ranking — The Starting Ten and the Ninety Earned

*Aug 3 2026. All 100 characters stack-ranked for the new-player experience. Rank 1–10
is the initial fleet; 11–100 is ordered by how much a player will want each one, which
is exactly the order that matters when deciding what an achievement is worth.*

## What this ranking optimizes

A character is just a name, a face, a species, a swatch and a boat — no stats for the
player. So the starting ten is a **merchandising problem, not a balance problem**:

1. **Every player finds themself in ten seconds.** The slate covers the personalities
   people actually shop with: *I'm here to win. I'm chill. I'm weird. I'm a sailor.
   I'm adorable. I'm scrappy. I like bright colors. I'm an old salt. I'm happy.*
2. **Instant species recognition.** Otter, shark, lobster — no one asks "what is a
   humpback chub" on their first screen. (Chisel's day comes at Redrock.)
3. **Ten distinct hulls on the water.** The starter fleet races itself, so the ten
   swatches must read at a glance mid-race.
4. **All eight archetypes in the opposing fleet.** The player takes no stats, but the
   nine boats they race against are the tutorial: one metronome, one rocket, one
   bully teaches more about racing than any help screen.
5. **No venue witnesses.** Venues are named for their characters (Sockeye
   Run/Slipstream, Gatorgrass/Chomp, Spoonbill Flats/Petal…). Those characters are
   pre-sold achievements — putting one in the starter slate spends it twice.
6. **Hold back some superstars.** Ripple, Fathom, Wiggle are deliberately NOT in the
   ten. The first few achievements must feel like gifts, and a gift is only as good
   as who's inside. If the ten most-wanted characters are all free, the other ninety
   are a completionist's chore.

**Fleet math.** Hulls: blue, black, pink, red, white, orange, yellow, purple, tan,
gray — ten distinct swatches, no two adjacent on the water. Species: mammal ×3
(otter, walrus, seal), shark, crustacean ×2 (crab, lobster), bird, monotreme, fish,
cephalopod. Archetypes: all eight present — bully ×2 (Bruce, Pinch), metronome ×2
(Cheer, Glide), one each of shift, gambler, rocket, leech, freight, corner. The
doubles are the right doubles: two bullies make the start line feel contested from
race one, and two metronomes give the beginner someone beatable to pace against.

### Why each one

1. **Bixby (Sea Otter, shift)** — The Mario. Otters are the most universally adored
   marine mammal, and "relaxed veteran who instinctively finds perfect wind" is the
   exact energy a new player wants on their side of the screen. Royal blue with a
   gold kite is the classic yacht-club livery. As an opponent, the shift archetype
   quietly demonstrates that the wind has a pattern worth reading.
2. **Bruce (Great White, bully)** — The Bowser. Every roster needs the character the
   competitive picker grabs without scrolling, and a black-hulled great white named
   Bruce (the *Jaws* mechanical shark, the *Nemo* support-group shark) is that
   character. As an opponent he's the fleet's menace — slow to restart, terrifying
   in a blow — which makes beating him the first story players tell.
3. **Cheer (Pom Pom Crab, metronome)** — The brand ambassador. A tiny crab that
   waves pom poms is the most *Salty Critter Yacht Club* thing in the whole file:
   happy, bright, adorable and a crustacean all at once. Pink hull, cyan-and-gold
   kite — the loudest boat in the starter fleet. Steady metronome AI means the
   friendliest face is also the beatable rival beginners measure themselves against.
4. **Pinch (American Lobster, bully)** — The club mascot. It's a *salty critter*
   yacht club; a red lobster boat belongs on the poster. Fills the scrappy slot —
   an aggressive line fighter who's mortal everywhere else, teaching new players
   both that starts matter and that fights aren't free.
5. **Glide (Wandering Albatross, metronome)** — The sailor's pick. The albatross is
   the most storied bird in sailing lore, and the white hull with a deep blue kite
   is the understated choice for players who came for the sailing, not the
   critters. "Patient perfectionist who never blunders" is who every real racer
   believes they are.
6. **Wobble (Platypus, gambler)** — The weirdo. When evolution's punchline shows up
   in an orange boat with a purple kite, the "I refuse to pick a normal animal"
   player is served on screen one. Gambler AI sends him banging corners, so every
   race gets one boat with a story — hero or cautionary tale.
7. **Sunshine (Mahi-Mahi, rocket)** — The bright one. Mahi are real bluewater
   gamefish that look like they were colored by a five-year-old with the good
   markers — yellow hull, green kite, zero apology. Fills the bright-colors slot
   and gives the fleet its rocket: explosive when the gun fires, catchable when it
   matters.
8. **Tangle (Common Octopus, leech)** — The clever one. The octopus is the animal
   the internet decided is a genius, and purple-with-green is a color pair nobody
   else in the ten owns. His leech AI is the tutorial on dirty air: the first time
   a player wonders why their boat slowed down, Tangle is why.
9. **Whiskers (Walrus, freight)** — The old salt. The mustache does the talking.
   Tan hull, burgundy kite — a vintage wooden-boat palette that reads as "has been
   sailing since before you were born." The freight archetype makes him the
   immovable object downwind, which is exactly what an old salt should be.
   (Snap wanted this slot; two grumpy elders in ten is one too many, and Glacier
   Sound doesn't need the walrus the way it needs the polar bear.)
10. **Roll (Harbor Seal, corner)** — The adorable one. Harbor seals are the puppies
    of the sea, and "playful feints hiding brutal positioning" is charm with teeth.
    Gray hull with a gold kite rounds out the palette, and his corner archetype
    completes the eight-archetype set — the fleet's mark-rounding artist.

## 11–25 · First-wave achievements — superstars held back on purpose

These are starter-quality characters deliberately reserved so the first unlocks feel
like gifts, not consolation prizes. Cheap, early, loud.

11. **Ripple (Bottlenose Dolphin, shift)** — The most universally loved sea animal.
    The first achievement every player completes (finish your first regatta) should
    hand them the dolphin. Day-one gift, maximum grin.
12. **Fathom (Orca, freight)** — The kid-magnet apex. Priced as prestige: win your
    first championship. Black-and-blue livery makes victory look the part.
13. **Wiggle (Axolotl, gambler)** — The internet's favorite amphibian. An early
    surprise unlock (first capsize-free race? first podium?) lands hardest with the
    younger crowd.
14. **Snap (Snapping Turtle, metronome)** — The written-for-it old salt ("grouchy old
    salty sailor who likes to beat the young whippersnappers"). Earn him by beating
    a veteran rival — Whiskers — in a head-to-head finish.
15. **Pebble (Adelie Penguin, metronome)** — Top-five adorable in the file, but she's
    Glacier Sound's — penguin as polar venue unlock is a promise players understand
    on sight.
16. **Dart (Kingfisher, rocket)** — "Pure speed, very competitive" in cyan and
    orange. Win-streak achievement: the character rewards exactly the personality
    that wants him.
17. **Jester (Clownfish, leech)** — The single most recognizable fish on Earth.
    Reef/lagoon unlock — finding Nemo is the achievement.
18. **Anchor (Sea Turtle, metronome)** — Beloved, mellow, brutally consistent.
    Consistency achievement: finish top-five in every race of a regatta.
19. **Skim (Flying Fish, rocket)** — The launch-speed showoff. Top-speed or

20. **Crush (Mantis Shrimp, corner)** — Neon teal-and-pink showstopper with the

21. **Strut (Flamingo, metronome)** — Pink style icon; Flamingo Reach's witness when
    that venue lands, a style achievement until then.
22. **Zing (Flying Squirrel, rocket)** — Hyperactive chaos in purple and yellow.
    Comeback achievement: win after rounding the first mark last.
23. **Torch (Fire Salamander, rocket)** — The hot rod. Emberfall Isle pack, or
    "lead every leg of a race" until the volcano ships.
24. **Splat (Blobfish, gambler)** — The meme legend. The gag achievement: finish
    last in every race of a regatta and he's yours. "Looks doomed, always
    survives" — the loss that gives you something.
25. **Scuttle (Hermit Crab, gambler)** — Scrappy underdog. Contact-free chaos
    achievement: finish a crowded race without a single penalty.

## Notes for the achievement design pass (next)

- **Ranks 11–25 are the pacing budget.** Spend them in the first two hours of play.
  One unlock per early milestone, dolphin first.
- **Ranks 26–45 map 1:1 onto venues** already specced in `venues.md`. Each venue
  should grant its witness on first win and its pack-mates on deeper mastery.
- **Ranks 46–70 are sets** — shark pack, reef pack, jelly pack, bluewater pack.
  Sets want a collection UI moment and a capstone reward for completion.
- **Muninn (56) is the only mechanically special character** (`windFast` trait) and
  should be the final or near-final unlock — the roster's Mew.
- **Every earned character joins the opponent pool**, so unlock order is also
  difficulty curve: the starter ten's stat spreads are moderate; monsters like
  Mistral, Razor and Muninn arriving late keeps the fleet growing meaner as the
  player grows better.
