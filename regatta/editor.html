Sculpt drags nearby vertices along with the cursor — strongest at the centre, nothing at the rim. Smooth pulls them toward the midpoint of their neighbours, flattening wobble. The shaded disc is the reach; the dots are the vertices that will move, sized by how much. [ ] resizes.

A mark may carry a buoy, a can, or nothing at all — a position the race marks with an indicator, which is what an island rounding or a transit wants. Renaming is optional: leave the box empty and the name above it is used, and it keeps up as the course changes. Marks and gates live here; the Route is only the order you sail them, so the same gate can appear in it more than once. Deleting happens HERE — select on the map or in the list and press Delete. A gate whose marks are deleted goes with them.

A rect matches a painted map exactly. Inset it so land continues past the sailing limit — otherwise a sailor at the edge sees the world end.

Wind regions

There is no base wind: a region states the wind THERE, and one region over the whole course is how you say "steady breeze everywhere". Regions may overlap and are averaged, not added — two of them 40° apart give a curve at the same strength, not a squall. Leave speed blank to keep the venue's own range (and its race-to-race variety); set it to pin a number.

Water colour
A live patch of this venue's water, drawn by the game's own renderer — ripple lattice, caustics and depth ramp included — so the colours are the colours you will sail on rather than four swatches that only suggest them.
Current regions

A current region carries a FLOW — an absolute direction and speed — rather than a modifier, because a patch of water either has a stream running through it or it does not. Regions add as vectors, so two overlapping streams give a resultant. Flow ° is the direction the water is GOING. The water itself is not an object: it is wherever land and the arena are not.

per drag
% size spread

Drag to place a floe — the drag sets its size and the outline is generated, so no two look alike. Raise scatter to drop several inside the drag instead of one, which is how you set density. Click one to select, drag it to move, Delete to remove; reshape it vertex by vertex in Vertices. Position and shape are yours; each race gives every floe a fresh drift, spin and wander, so playthroughs stay unique.

This venue has no venue-specific objects to place. Effects like snowfall and aurora come from the venue definition in the code.

Scale everything
%

Scales land, marks, rounding zones and the arena together, keeping the start line at its fleet-sized length. Replaces editing MASK_WORLD. Prop sprites do not scale.

Measure

A boat drawn to scale answers "does this fit" directly: drag its hull to move it, drag the ring at its bow to turn it. The dashed circle is the three-length RRS zone, which is what a mark rounding has to accommodate. Drag to measure one leg; Shift-click to extend it into a path and get per-leg lengths plus the total. Reads metres, boat lengths (11 m, a J111) and the time that distance implies. A measurement stays on screen so you can edit against it — Esc or the button clears it.

The estimate measures the sailable path — hull-width, around the land — and prices each leg with the game's own J111 polar: the best VMG toward that leg's bearing, which is what tacking upwind and gybing downwind cost. It does not model manoeuvre losses or traffic, so it is a floor for a well-sailed boat. Blank prestart means the player's own setting (30 s default). Boats still sailing when the limit expires are scored DNF. Target band 3–5 min.
