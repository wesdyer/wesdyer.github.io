Course
Timing
The estimate measures the sailable path — hull-width, around the land — and prices each leg with the game's own J111 polar. It does not model manoeuvre losses or traffic, so it is a floor for a well-sailed boat. Blank prestart means the player's own setting. Boats still sailing when the limit expires are scored DNF.

Selected gate

A mark may carry a buoy, a can, or nothing at all — a position the race marks with an indicator, which is what an island rounding or a transit wants. Renaming is optional: leave the box empty and the name above it is used, and it keeps up as the course changes. Marks and gates live here; the Route is only the order you sail them, so the same gate can appear in it more than once. Deleting happens HERE — select on the map or in the list and press Delete. A gate whose marks are deleted goes with them.

Wind regions

A region states the wind THERE — where it comes from, as a compass bearing, 0° being north at the top of the screen. Outside every region there is no wind at all, so a course needs its water covered; the checks say so when it is not. Regions may overlap and are averaged, not added — two of them 40° apart give a curve at the same strength, not a squall. Leave speed blank to keep the venue's own range (and its race-to-race variety); set it to pin a number. Wind field at the top shows the result.

Water colour
A live patch of this venue's water, drawn by the game's own renderer — ripple lattice, caustics and depth ramp included — so the colours are the colours you will sail on rather than four swatches that only suggest them.
Current regions

A current region carries a FLOW — an absolute direction and speed — rather than a modifier, because a patch of water either has a stream running through it or it does not. Regions add as vectors, so two overlapping streams give a resultant. Toward ° is the compass bearing the water is GOING (a current is named by where it sets, unlike a wind). The water itself is not an object: it is wherever land and the arena are not.

Drag to place a floe — the drag sets its size and the outline is generated, so no two look alike. Raise scatter to drop several inside the drag instead of one, which is how you set density. Click one to select, drag it to move, Delete to remove; reshape it vertex by vertex in Vertices. Position and shape are yours; each race gives every floe a fresh drift, spin and wander, so playthroughs stay unique.

Measure

A boat drawn to scale answers "does this fit" directly: drag it to move, Cmd/Ctrl+drag to turn it — the same controls as a land shape. The dashed circle is the three-length RRS zone, which is what a mark rounding has to accommodate. Drag to measure one leg; Shift-click to extend it into a path and get per-leg lengths plus the total. Reads metres, boat lengths (11 m, a J111) and the time that distance implies. A measurement stays on screen so you can edit against it — Esc or the button clears it.
